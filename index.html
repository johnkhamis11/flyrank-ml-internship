CTR & Engagement Opportunity Scoring

Author: John Khamis

Abstract

This research investigates search discoverability by identifying high-impression pages suffering from below-average Click-Through Rates (CTR). Using DuckDB and baseline heuristic rules on FlyRank's search dataset, we built a scoring engine to rank content optimization opportunities. Our primary rule isolates pages ranking in top positions (< 5.0) with low relative engagement. The model outputs prioritized action codes to guide metadata and content refreshes. Testing confirms the baseline score reliably flags high-leverage pages without target leakage.

1. Problem Statement

SEO and content teams often struggle to prioritize which high-volume pages require metadata updates. This work creates a decision-support system to flag pages with high visibility but under-captured intent.

2. Data & Methodology

Data was processed using FlyRank's ML Internship warehouse release. We defined features including impressions, position, and baseline CTR. Pages were filtered to eliminate private queries and low-data noise.

3. Results & Recommendations

The scoring pipeline successfully generated a ranked action queue (written to work/outputs/baseline_action_score.csv). Key recommendations focus on optimizing Meta Titles and Snippets for high-impression, low-CTR queries.
